## Purpose
Australian SMEs are adopting Generative AI faster than they are building governance for it. Existing guidance (NIST CSF 2.0, NIST AI RMF, OAIC, OWASP, ISO/IEC 42001) is comprehensive but fragmented — organisations lack a simple, repeatable way to measure how ready they actually are. This project designs, builds and evaluates a browser-based readiness assessment tool that closes that gap.

## Aim
To design, develop and evaluate a practical Generative AI Cybersecurity and Privacy Readiness Assessment Framework that enables Australian SMEs to measure control maturity, identify priority risks, and produce a targeted improvement roadmap for the secure use of external GenAI tools.

## Scope
**In scope:** employee use of externally provided GenAI tools in Australian organisational settings; a 30-question control library across six domains; a scoring model with evidence caps and critical control gates; a browser-based (HTML/CSS/JS, no backend) prototype; evaluation via two synthetic organisational scenarios and expert reviewer feedback.

**Out of scope:** legal advice, compliance certification, security testing of commercial AI providers, training a new AI model, or deploying a production enterprise platform.

## Objectives (SMART, mapped to Part B milestones)
1. By end of Week 3 — traceable control library, 30 questions across 6 domains, mapped to source frameworks.
2. By end of Week 5 — four-level maturity model (0–4 scoring), evidence requirements, domain weights, critical control gates.
3. By end of Week 7 — working browser prototype: domain/overall scoring, 6+ priority gaps identified, prioritised roadmap generated, no data uploaded or stored.
4. By end of Week 9 — evaluated via deterministic scoring tests, two synthetic scenarios, and structured feedback from 2+ reviewers, targeting an average clarity/usefulness rating of ≥4/5.
